Common questions

My alarm didn't ring. Make sure WakeStar is allowed to schedule alarms: open the iOS Settings app → WakeStar and check that alarms are allowed. Then open WakeStar once so it can reschedule your alarms.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → WakeStar Pro, then tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought WakeStar Pro on another iPhone. Open WakeStar → Settings → Manage Subscription and tap Restore past purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data? See our privacy policy under “Your choices”.
