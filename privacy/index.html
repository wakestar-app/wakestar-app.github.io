WakeStar Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

  • WakeStar has no accounts. We never ask for your name.
  • Camera, microphone and motion are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • We collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the app. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • If you allow location, your coordinates are sent to our weather provider to show local weather.
  • No ads. We never sell your data or track you across other apps.

Who we are

WakeStar ("we") is an alarm app for iPhone. If you have any question about this policy or your data, email blazefrostrider@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Most of WakeStar works entirely on your device. The following is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Camera. Exercise missions such as squats and push-ups analyze the camera image on your iPhone to count reps. No photos or video are recorded or uploaded.
  • Microphone and speech. Speaking missions are recognized on your device. No audio or transcripts leave it.
  • Motion. Step and shake missions read your iPhone's motion sensors on the device.
  • Screen Time. If you use App Lock, the apps you pick are stored as private tokens created by Apple. We can't see which apps they are, and they never leave your device.
  • Your content. Alarm labels, sounds you import, QR codes you register, affirmations you type and your wake-up history are stored only on your iPhone.

What we collect, and why

Anonymous usage analytics. To learn which features help people wake up, the app sends usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog. Examples: an alarm was created or rang, a mission was completed, a screen of the setup was finished, the paywall was shown. Events include the alarm and bedtime times you set, your settings and permission choices, your device model, iOS version, language and time zone. Each install gets a random ID that is not connected to your name, email, Apple ID or device identifiers. You can see this ID in Settings → About. Like any internet service, PostHog can see the IP address your request comes from. We don't use it to identify you.

You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Share Anonymous Analytics.

Weather and location. If you allow location access, WakeStar sends your device's coordinates to WeatherAPI.com to get your local forecast, and uses Apple's location services to look up the name of your town. Your location and town name are saved on your device so the forecast can refresh. Your location is not sent to our analytics. If you don't allow location, the app works without weather.

Purchases. Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We never see your payment card or Apple ID. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipts and an anonymous ID it creates.

Feedback you send us. If you send a message from Settings (Report a Bug or Request a Feature), we receive your message, your app version and device model, and your email address only if you choose to give one so we can reply. Feedback is sent only when you tap Send. Because sending it is your choice, it is delivered even if analytics is turned off. Feedback is sent separately from analytics and isn't connected to it, so your email is never tied to your app usage.

What we never do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't combine your data with data from other sources.

How long we keep data

We keep usage analytics only as long as it is useful for improving WakeStar, and we keep feedback messages only as long as we need them to respond and fix the problem you reported. Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it or delete the app.

Your choices

  • Turn off analytics in Settings → Share Anonymous Analytics.
  • Change permissions for location, camera, microphone and motion at any time in the iOS Settings app.
  • Delete your data. Deleting WakeStar removes everything stored on your iPhone, including the random install ID. To have the analytics we hold deleted, email blazefrostrider@gmail.com with the install ID from Settings → About. To have feedback deleted, tell us the email address you used or roughly when you sent it. We'll delete it and confirm by email.
  • Manage subscriptions in the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, and to object to how we use it. Email us to use any of these rights.

Children

WakeStar is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has sent us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
